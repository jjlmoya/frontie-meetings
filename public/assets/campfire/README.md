# Campfire song assets

Campfire is available in the shared video app at `/?config=campfire`. It renders live from the layered scene assets, so it does not need a separate preview video; the master mix drives playback and aligned stems drive Frontie's mouth and guitar animation.

- `audio/`: full MP3 used by the video player.
- `scripts/assets/campfire-stems/`: separate aligned tracks used to derive vocal, rhythm, and instrument cues. They stay in the source workspace and are not served to viewers. Keep their common start time; do not combine them into a second playback mix. Rebuild `animation-cues.json` with `python3 scripts/build-campfire-cues.py` after replacing stems.
- `animation-cues.json`: compact 50 Hz RMS envelopes generated from the vocal, drum, and guitar stems, trimmed to the decoded master MP3 length. Those are the three tracks the current video uses: vocal for mouth articulation, drums and guitar for scene and playing motion.
- `lyrics.json`: full on-screen lyrics with phrase start/end times. The Xavi and María event lines carry only the exact portrait `appearanceAt`; the portraits appear suddenly, with no warning blackout.
- `layers/`: transparent character and scene assets. Keep the complete scene separated into individual pieces under `layers/scene/` so it can be recomposed and animated without losing the reference composition.
- `layers/characters/`: Frontie puppet pieces use independent torso, seated legs, arms, lute, and tail. The eight large puppet layers are served as lossless WebP while their transparent PNG masters stay in `scripts/assets/campfire-character-layers/`; rebuild them with `node scripts/build-campfire-character-webp.js`. The fretting arm sits behind the lute while its transparent paw overlay sits above the neck, lowered together to grip the middle of the neck. For singing, `frontie-head-closed.webp` is the fixed face and `frontie-mouth-open-v10.png` is a transparent mouth-only overlay, shifted 11 px left on its 443x443 canvas to center the mouth beneath the nose; rebuild it from `scripts/assets/campfire-frontie-mouth-open-v9-source.png` with `node scripts/build-campfire-mouth-layer.js`. Eyes, brows, gaze, muzzle pads, whiskers, and face colors remain fixed. The 50 Hz cue clock opens above 0.2 RMS (0.1 for whispers or soft speech). It closes in vocal valleys against 90% of a recent peak that decays over 180 ms. Ordinary singing also gets a gentle 620 ms maximum open hold (680 ms for whispers and 640 ms for soft speech) plus 140 ms minimum closed and 160 ms minimum open intervals, preventing both a stuck jaw and frantic chatter; sustained screams keep their long open pose. Smoothing uses audio time so displays articulate consistently across refresh rates. Keep Xavi's tied long hair and María's headset/glasses in the transparent WebP screamer portraits; both are preloaded so the jump scares appear on time.
- `layers/scene/`: compressed painted forest and firepit layers, transparent star layer, full-canvas lossless WebP midground treeline and foreground canopy cutouts that let aircraft pass behind the tree silhouettes, four aligned painted flame frames constrained to the stone opening, two distant airplane passes, three separate falling-leaf sprites, and separate ground props (raven skull, brambles, spent candle). The forest builder removes the corrupted bottom strip and normalizes the canvas to 1672×941; the tree masks apply that same transform so the cutouts stay aligned for occlusion. The fire frames crossfade from the audio timeline instead of hard-swapping. Keep compositing order: forest backdrop, aircraft and loose scene details, midground treeline, then foreground canopies. Rebuild both tree cutouts from the original forest pixels with `node scripts/build-campfire-foreground-trees.js`. Rebuild the live flame loop from the four source poses in `scripts/assets/campfire-scene-iterations/` with `node scripts/build-campfire-natural-flames.js`; rebuild the earlier eight-frame study from `scripts/assets/campfire-flame-loop-sheet-v1.png` with `node scripts/build-campfire-flame-loop.js`. Rebuild the forest, stars, plane, leaves, and optimized WebP ground props with `node scripts/build-campfire-scene-details.js`. Rebuild the firepit base and foreground overlay together from `scripts/assets/campfire-scene-iterations/firepit-base-source.png` with `node scripts/build-campfire-firepit-front.js`.
- `layers/characters/xavi-screamer-ponytail.webp` is the active transparent Xavi scare portrait, retaining the existing composition with his long tied-back hair made visible. The unused previous portrait is archived at `scripts/assets/campfire-character-iterations/xavi-screamer-original.webp`, outside the public asset tree.
- `layers/scene/crescent-moon.png` and `constellation.png` are independent transparent sky accents. The rare shooting star is drawn as its own tiny CSS motion element; keep each detail restrained and behind the tree cutouts.
- Large PNG source layers, original ground-prop art, and unused scene experiments live in `scripts/assets/campfire-scene-iterations/`. Unused character drafts live in `scripts/assets/campfire-character-iterations/`; the video route serves only the optimized WebP and PNG layers it uses.

## Reusing this setup

1. Put the new master MP3 in `audio/` and its time-aligned stems in `scripts/assets/campfire-stems/`.
2. Keep stems at the same start time as the master. Generate 50 Hz cue envelopes with the script above.
3. Time every lyric line against the master in `lyrics.json`; leave instrumental gaps blank. Spell technical terms correctly even if the vocal uses phonetic spellings.
4. Build character and scene art as separate transparent raster pieces. Use a matched open/closed face pair; if the source faces differ outside the mouth, keep the closed head fixed and overlay only the mouth from the open frame. Animate limbs around fixed contact points.
5. Put character surprises on explicit lyric events. For a jump scare, show the portrait suddenly at the reveal timestamp; do not fade or cut the scene to black beforehand.
6. Preview the route at the reveal timestamps in desktop widescreen, and confirm lyric readability, mouth timing, layer order, and the limbs' contact with instruments.

## Campfire visual notes

- Keep lyrics high on screen with a dark outline and shadow. Never put them in a box, and keep them above the screamer portraits so the sung line stays visible.
- Use oversized bone-white lyrics (`clamp(2.05rem, 4.25vw, 4.15rem)`) with hard shadow and outline. Give selected fear and engineering phrases a brighter crimson heartbeat, a brief scale/tilt snap, and spectral echo; keep the words crisp and the line fully readable over each screamer.
- Make screeners arrive without a preceding fade or black screen. Show the portrait suddenly on its spoken name, and keep the lyric above it.
- Keep Frontie's lowered right fretting paw visibly gripping the middle of the lute neck. Tie small hand movement to guitar cues, and vary storytelling gestures by section instead of using a large constant bob or sway.
- Add small connected story details as transparent raster layers. Keep their movement intermittent and grounded in the forest or on the dirt; avoid emoji, insects, and unrelated clutter.
- Let the leaves drift down from the canopy along the outer sides of the stage, settle briefly on the dirt, and fade there. Keep aircraft distant above the treeline; none of these should cross the lyric line.
