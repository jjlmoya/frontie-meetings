# Frontie the Feline King

[Verse 1]
Gather ‘round the tavern table, shield your glowing screen,
Hear the tale of Frontie, king of every feline queen.
He stalks across the keyboard where the pull requests are laid,
Guardian of the codebase, in ancient fur arrayed.
